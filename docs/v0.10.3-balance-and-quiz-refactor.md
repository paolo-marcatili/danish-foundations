# v0.10.3 balance and quiz refactor

This commit is the first of two planned updates. It changes game balance and quiz variety only; the renderer/asset refactor remains separate.

## Balance

Level stat caps now start at 5 and increase by 5 per level.

```yaml
level 1: 5
level 2: 10
level 3: 15
```

A completed training session now gives exactly +1 to its linked attribute. Individual correct answers update memory and temporary session scoring, but they do not directly increase attributes. If the session has too many mistakes, it does not count and no stat is awarded.

Each level now requires 5 successfully completed training sessions at that level before the monster will accept the challenge. Extra training from earlier levels no longer automatically satisfies later level gates.

## Fight survival

The hard fight mistake cap has been removed. Instead, monster damage is balanced through Defense and Stamina:

- very low Defense/Stamina can lose from one mistake;
- maxed Defense/Stamina can survive about five mistakes and usually falls on the sixth;
- timeouts still hurt slightly more than ordinary wrong answers.

Monster HP is still protected by the 10-question minimum rule: the monster cannot die before ten answered questions, and after that HP <= 0 produces a victory.

## Quiz variants

Grammar now includes more variants:

- choose the correct Armenian sentence;
- choose the Italian meaning of an Armenian sentence;
- find the missing Armenian word;
- tap Armenian word chips in order.

The tap-in-order exercise is intentionally button-based instead of drag-and-drop to work better on mobile.
