# Graphics asset licensing

The original placeholder scenery, object, monster, station, and companion assets
created specifically for Hero Language Camp are released under CC0 1.0 / public
domain dedication by the project author.

The seven hero animation source sheets under
`sources/hero-animation-sheets/` were supplied separately by a contributor and
are imported without asserting a new license. Before redistributing a public
build or asset pack, confirm that the contributor has the necessary rights and
record the chosen license here. The normalized runtime copies inherit the same
rights as those source sheets.

The merged side-scroller backgrounds, scenery objects, training stations,
hero sheets and companion sheets in v0.11.6 were supplied through the project
contribution workflow. Their presence in a folder named `cc0-pixel-v10` does
not by itself establish a CC0 license. Before public redistribution, confirm
contributor rights and record the intended license for those files.

The v0.11.5 labyrinth images and the v0.11.6 monster concept/cutouts are
image-model-derived project assets that were normalized and edited for this
prototype. Record the project's selected redistribution license before treating
the complete art pack as an independently redistributable asset library.
